### Reaper MIDI Device Mapping
The mio XM presents 16 generic MIDI ports over its single USB-DAW connection (Windows/Reaper can't see through to what's plugged into DIN or HST ports individually, everything shows up as anonymous "MIDI IN/OUT" numbers - had to confirm each one empirically, watching for sound or the synth's own MIDI-in activity light). Default mio XM port order per iConnectivity's own docs: DIN 1-4, HST 1-8, Preset Selector, RSV 1-3 (16 total).

| MIDI IN/OUT # | mio Port | Synth | MIDI Channel |
|---------------|----------|-------|--------------|
| 1 | DIN 1 | Electribe ESX-1 → microKORG chain | Electribe: 13, microKORG: 15 |
| 2 | DIN 2 | Dave Smith Poly Evolver | 16 |
| 3 | DIN 3 | Alesis Micron | 1 |
| 4 | DIN 4 | Yamaha PSR-730 | 1 (probably) |
| 5 | HST 1 | Modal Argon8 | 1 (probably) |
| 6 | HST 2 | Moog Little Phatty Stage II | 11 |
| 7 | HST 3 | M-Audio Radium 49 | TBD |
| 8 | HST 4 | Unassigned (mio USB 4 open) | - |
| 9-12 | HST 5-8 | Nothing connected - reserved for a future USB MIDI expander/hub, not needed right now |
| 13 | Preset Selector | Not a synth - send it a message to remotely switch the mio XM's stored routing presets from the DAW without opening Auracle |
| 14-16 | RSV 1-3 | Reserved, no default routing, fully open for custom use via Auracle X |

- Controller: [Novation Launchpad Mini](https://us.novationmusic.com/products/launchpad-mini)
- DAWs: [Ableton Live Lite](https://www.ableton.com/en/products/live-lite/) and [Reaper](https://www.reaper.fm/)
